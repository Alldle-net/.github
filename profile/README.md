[Alldle](https://www.alldle.net) is a directory of the daily guessing games people play every morning: Wordle, Worldle and Connections, and thousands of games made by independent creators, from words and maps to music, sports and trivia. Each game has its own page, filed by category, and by universe for games about a franchise. Free to browse, no signup.

### For game makers

- **List your game.** Send its link from [alldle.net/submit](https://www.alldle.net/submit), or [add it from your creator account](https://docs.alldle.net/listing/add-your-game/) to describe it yourself.
- **Own its page.** [Claim a game](https://docs.alldle.net/listing/claim-a-game/) that is already listed to edit it and see who plays it and whether they come back, with the [Alldle SDK](https://docs.alldle.net/sdk/overview/).
- **Work from your AI assistant.** The [Alldle MCP server](https://docs.alldle.net/mcp/overview/) lets Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor list your game and read how it is doing.
- **Get listed everywhere else too.** [dle-submit-mcp](https://github.com/Alldle-net/dle-submit-mcp) sends your game to the directories that list daily games, prepares the rest, and remembers where it has been.
